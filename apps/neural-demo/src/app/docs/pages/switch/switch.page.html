A native, Signal-first boolean control for immediate settings, with accessible switch semantics, readonly discovery, Field composition, and complete headless ownership.

Accessibility

Label activation, Space, focus, disabled, required, validation, and form submission come from the native input. Readonly is represented with aria-readonly and blocks changes without removing focus.
